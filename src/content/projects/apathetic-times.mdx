Many people opt out of following Congress because the coverage is stressful and written to provoke.
Apathetic Times is our attempt at a calmer way back in, one that gives people a sense that their
view counts.

I'm building it with my colleague [Ben Goldin](https://bengoldin.com/). He sets the product and
design direction, and I lead the engineering.

The app picks one report a day from the Congressional Research Service, Congress's nonpartisan
research group. You read or listen to a short brief on it in under five minutes, then share your
take: what matters to you, and what outcome you'd want. You land somewhere from a hard no to an
emphatic yes, and talk it over with friends in a shared room.

## How it works

Each day a scheduled job pulls the latest reports from Congress and automatically picks one, using
guidelines we set. It parses the report and hands it to Claude, which writes the brief, the
discussion prompt and a script for the audio version. Gemini's text-to-speech then reads the script
aloud. It all runs on Cloudflare: the daily job, the database, and the chat rooms, which update live
as friends vote and comment.

We built it for the web so we could iterate quickly and reach anyone, whatever phone they have. We
learned that from our first version, a native iOS app: every change waited on Apple's review, and we
couldn't hear from anyone on Android.

Today it's where Ben, our friends and I trade takes on whatever Congress is up to, and I come away
knowing a little more each time. We're still building, so it can reach more of the people who've
tuned out and give them some say in a chaotic government.
