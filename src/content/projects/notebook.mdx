My desk used to be a mess of sticky notes and notepad pages, and most notes apps didn't help: they
ask you to organize before you write, with folders, tags and pages. Notebook is for anyone who just
wants one simple, private place for their day. I design and build it myself, and I use it every day.

Each day is a plain ruled list of notes and tasks that you edit in place, like lines in a text
editor. Alongside it are your habits, the tasks you've pinned, and, on Mac and iPhone, the day's
events from your own calendars. At the end of each week, AI can write you a short recap, with zero
data retention. Your notes work offline and stay private.

## How it works

Notes are saved to a database on your device, so everything is instant and works offline. In the
background they sync through [Starling](/projects/starling), my own sync library, which encrypts
everything on the device first, so the server only relays data it can't read. Weekly recaps are the
one time readable text leaves the device, and only when you ask: it goes to an AI service that never
learns who sent it.

It's one app in three places: on the web at [notebook.byearlybird.com](https://notebook.byearlybird.com),
so anyone can try it without installing anything, and as Mac and iPhone apps built with
[Tauri](https://tauri.app).

The sticky notes are gone from my desk.
