## What it is

Most notes apps ask you to organize before you write: folders, tags, pages. Notebook skips all of
that. Each day is a plain ruled list of notes and tasks that you edit in place, and a separate list
holds what you want to get done this week.

It works offline. Signing in to sync across devices is optional, and your notes stay private when
they sync.

## How it's built

- **Local first.** Notes are saved to a database on your device, so everything is instant and
  works offline. Sync happens in the background.
- **Encrypted sync.** Sync runs on [Starling](/projects/starling), my own sync library. Notes are
  encrypted on the device before they leave it, so the server never sees them.
- **Built for more than one device.** Edits from your phone and laptop merge piece by piece, so
  moving a note on one never undoes rewording it on the other.
- **On the web.** It installs from the browser on any device and opens straight to today.
