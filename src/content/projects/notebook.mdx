## What it is

Most notes apps ask you to organize before you write: folders, tags, pages. Notebook skips all of
that. Each day is a plain ruled list of notes and tasks that you edit in place, and a separate list
holds what you want to get done this week. Press + to add a row, Enter for the next one, and Tab to
nest it.

It works offline, needs no account, and keeps your notes private even when they sync.

## How it's built

- **Local first.** Entries live in IndexedDB on your device, and the app works fully signed out.
  Every edit is saved locally before anything touches the network.
- **End-to-end encrypted sync.** Sync runs on [Starling](https://github.com/nickrmurphy/starling),
  my own sync library. Everything that syncs is encrypted with an account key that only your
  passphrase unlocks, so the server never sees your notes.
- **Cloudflare backend.** Each account's synced data lives in its own SQLite-backed Durable Object,
  accounts live in D1, and sign-in uses emailed one-time codes.
- **Conflict-friendly data model.** Rows use fractional rank keys, so dragging or nesting an entry
  writes only that entry, and edits on two devices merge field by field.
- **PWA.** It installs on a phone and opens straight to today.
