Notebook is a minimal daily notebook. Each day is a ruled list of notes and tasks that you
edit in place. Press `+` to open a row, Enter to start the next one, and Tab to nest it. A
separate list holds what you want to get done this week.

## Why I built it

I wanted a notes app that was a plain list per day, with nothing to organize and no folders.
It also had to work offline and keep my notes private.

## How it works

- It's local first. Entries are stored on the device and the app works fully signed out.
- Signing in with an emailed code and a passphrase turns on sync through
  [Starling](https://github.com/nickrmurphy/starling), my end-to-end encrypted sync library
  running on Cloudflare Workers.
- Rows use fractional rank keys, so dragging or nesting an entry writes only that entry. When
  two devices change the same field, the later edit wins.
- It's a PWA, so it installs on a phone and opens straight to today.

## What I learned

Most of the work went into sync edge cases: an entry whose parent was deleted on another device,
or two entries indented under each other. Making those entries show up at the top level instead
of disappearing kept the app trustworthy.
