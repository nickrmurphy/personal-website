Trailhead is a small web app that answers one question: _where should I hike this weekend?_
You tell it how far you're willing to drive and how long you want to be on the trail, and it
suggests a handful of routes with current conditions and the best time to leave.

## Why I built it

Most hiking apps are built for people who live next to mountains. Around Chicago, the good
trails are scattered across three states and the deciding factor is usually the drive, not the
elevation. I wanted something that started from my front door instead of from the trail.

## How it works

- Trail data comes from OpenStreetMap and is refreshed nightly.
- Drive times are calculated from your location, so results are ranked by total time out.
- Weather and daylight are checked against your planned start so it won't send you out at dusk.

## What I learned

Keeping the scope tiny was the best decision. The first version only supported a single
question and a list of five results, and that turned out to be most of the value.
