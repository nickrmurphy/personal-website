Pantry is a grocery list for households. Everyone in the house adds to the same list, and
items you've marked as stocked stop showing up until they run low again.

## Why I built it

Our list lived in a group chat, which meant buying three jars of peanut butter in one week.
I wanted the list to remember what we already had, not just what someone asked for.

## How it works

- Lists sync in real time across phones, so two people can shop the same list.
- Checking an item off moves it to the pantry with a rough "runs out in" estimate.
- When an estimate passes, the item quietly comes back to the list.

## What I learned

Offline support mattered more than any feature. Grocery stores have terrible signal, so the
app had to work fully without a connection and sync when it came back.
