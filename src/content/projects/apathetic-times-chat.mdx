Every day, Apathetic Times picks one report from the Congressional Research Service, Congress's
nonpartisan research group, and turns it into a briefing you can read in under five minutes.
The chat app adds a room around it: you and a few friends read the same briefing, vote on it,
and talk it over.

## Why I built it

Keeping up with Congress usually means wading through outrage. CRS reports are calm and
thorough, but they're long. Reading one short, neutral briefing a day is easier when other
people are reading it too.

## How it works

- Each day the newsroom service, a set of Cloudflare Workflows, ingests new CRS reports, picks
  one, and has Claude summarize it into a short briefing.
- Rooms run on Durable Objects, so messages and votes reach everyone in the room right away.
  D1 stores the briefings, rooms, and history.
- Voting is a row of five faces, from "No way" to "Totally", so you can react to a policy
  without writing a paragraph.
- There are no accounts. You pick a username, share a room link, and the app installs as a PWA.

## What I learned

The conversation is the reason to come back. A briefing on its own is easy to skip; one your
friends are already arguing about isn't.
