## What it is

Keeping up with what the government is doing is harder than it should be. Most options are either
engaging but slanted, or balanced but dry. Apathetic Times aims for objective and engaging.

Each day it takes one report from the Congressional Research Service, Congress's nonpartisan
research group, and turns it into a briefing you can read in under five minutes. It has no spin
and never mentions political parties. You and a few friends share a room, vote for or against the
day's statement on a scale from "No way" to "Totally", and talk it over.

## How it's built

- **A daily report pipeline.** Every morning a set of Cloudflare Workflows finds new reports from
  Congress, pulls out their text, and summarizes each one. Every step retries on its own, so one bad
  report never holds up the rest.
- **Briefings written with Claude.** Claude picks the day's report and writes the briefing in a fixed
  structure, so the app always gets content it knows how to show.
- **Real-time rooms.** Each room lives in its own Durable Object on Cloudflare, so messages and
  votes reach everyone in the room instantly.
- **No accounts.** You pick a username and share a link, and the app installs from the browser.
