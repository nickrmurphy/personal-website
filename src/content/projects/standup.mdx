Standup Digest collects the async standup updates a team posts in Slack and, at a set time
each morning, posts a short summary: what shipped, what's in progress, and who's blocked.

## Why I built it

Async standups are great until the thread gets long. Managers skim, blockers get buried, and
the people who need to help never see them. A summary that leads with blockers fixed that.

## How it works

- The bot watches a single channel and groups replies by person.
- A language model pulls out shipped work, current focus and blockers from each update.
- Blockers are listed first and mention the person most likely to help.

## What I learned

The summary was only trusted once every line linked back to the original message. People
wanted a shortcut, not a replacement.
